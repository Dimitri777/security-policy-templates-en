## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information assets belonging to **[Company Name]** and to ensure compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, temporary staff, and third parties who have access to **[Company Name]** information systems, data or facilities.

## 3. Policy Statements

3.1 Information is a critical asset of the organization and must be protected in accordance with its classification.

3.2 Access to information systems and data shall be granted on the principle of least privilege and need-to-know.

3.3 All users are responsible for protecting the information assets they use and for reporting security incidents immediately.

3.4 Security controls shall be implemented, monitored and regularly reviewed based on risk assessment.

3.5 Non-compliance with this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract, and may also lead to legal consequences.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Senior Management** — provides leadership and resources for information security.
- **CISO / Information Security Team** — develops, maintains and oversees the implementation of the information security management system.
- **Managers** — ensure that their teams understand and comply with security policies.
- **All Users** — follow security policies and report incidents.
