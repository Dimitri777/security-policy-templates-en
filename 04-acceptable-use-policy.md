# Acceptable Use Policy

**Document ID:** ISP-004  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** [Date]  
**Owner:** [CISO / HR]

## 1. Purpose

To define acceptable and unacceptable use of **[Company Name]** information systems, networks, devices and data.

## 2. Scope

All employees, contractors and other users of company systems and devices.

## 3. Acceptable Use

Users may use company systems for legitimate business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted provided it:
- does not interfere with work duties,
- does not consume significant resources,
- does not violate any other policy or law.

## 4. Prohibited Activities

Users must **not**:
- Access, download or distribute illegal, offensive, discriminatory or pornographic material
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to systems or data
- Install unauthorized software or hardware
- Disable or bypass security controls
- Share credentials
- Use company resources for personal commercial activities without approval
- Engage in activities that could harm the reputation of the company

## 5. Monitoring

**[Company Name]** reserves the right to monitor the use of its systems in accordance with applicable law and internal procedures.

## 6. Consequences

Violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action, including termination, and may be reported to law enforcement where appropriate.
