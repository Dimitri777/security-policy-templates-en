# Incident Response Policy

**Document ID:** ISP-006  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** [Date]  
**Owner:** [CISO]

## 1. Purpose

To establish a consistent and effective approach to the detection, response, recovery and learning from information security incidents.

## 2. Scope

All security incidents affecting **[Company Name]** information assets, systems or data.

## 3. Definitions

**Security Incident** — any event that compromises (or is suspected of compromising) the confidentiality, integrity or availability of information or systems.

## 4. Policy Statements

4.1 All employees must report suspected security incidents immediately through the defined channels: **[email / ticket system / phone]**.

4.2 The Incident Response Team (IRT) is responsible for coordinating the response according to the Incident Response Plan.

4.3 Incidents shall be classified by severity and handled with appropriate priority and escalation.

4.4 Evidence shall be preserved where necessary for investigation or legal purposes.

4.5 After major incidents a post-incident review (lessons learned) shall be conducted and improvements implemented.

4.6 Regulatory notification requirements (e.g. data breach notifications) shall be complied with within the required timeframes.
