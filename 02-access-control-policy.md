# Access Control Policy

**Document ID:** ISP-002  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** [Date]  
**Owner:** [CISO]

## 1. Purpose

To ensure that access to information systems and data is granted only to authorized individuals and only to the extent necessary for the performance of their duties.

## 2. Scope

All information systems, applications, networks and data owned or managed by **[Company Name]**.

## 3. Policy Statements

3.1 Access shall be granted based on the principles of **least privilege** and **need-to-know**.

3.2 User accounts must be unique and traceable to an individual. Shared accounts are prohibited unless explicitly approved and monitored.

3.3 Access rights shall be reviewed at least annually and upon change of role or termination of employment/contract.

3.4 Privileged (administrative) access shall be strictly controlled, logged and reviewed more frequently.

3.5 Remote access shall require multi-factor authentication.

3.6 Access requests must follow a formal process and be approved by the appropriate data/system owner.

3.7 Upon termination or role change, access rights must be revoked or modified in a timely manner (target: same business day).

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

- **System / Data Owners** — approve access requests and define access requirements.
- **IT / Identity Management** — implement and manage accounts and entitlements.
- **Information Security** — define standards, monitor privileged access, perform access reviews.
- **Managers** — request access for their staff and notify of leavers/movers.
